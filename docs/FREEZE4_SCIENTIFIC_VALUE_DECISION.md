# Freeze 4 Scientific-Value Decision

Decision: **retain_full_simulation_section**

The decision was recorded after the Freeze 3/4 differential audit and before manuscript reconstruction. A full simulation section is retained only when at least one prespecified U3 organizational-factor range is at least 0.05, exceeds 2 times its largest level-specific simulation MCSE, and the conditional-review denominator is interpretable and reported.

| U3 factor | Range | Maximum level MCSE | Range/MCSE | Gate |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| access_barrier | 0.025775 | 0.001238 | 20.8 | fail |
| retrievability | 0.214823 | 0.001206 | 178.1 | pass |
| staff_access | 0.000764 | 0.000988 | 0.8 | fail |

Retrievability qualifies. Its core range is 0.214823; the access-barrier and staff-access margins remain disclosed rather than promoted.

The core U3 conditional-review denominator contains 44,381 requests, 42,098 timely completions, and 20 zero-request family-replications out of 3,240. Every one of the 108 designed families has a positive total request denominator. The resulting request-weighted timely-review rate is 0.948559.

The latency sensitivity remains in the evidence packet. Its retrievability range is 0.214591; this is supporting robustness information, not a second value-gate search.

This gate controls prominence only. It does not validate the simulation against historical Robodebt or establish welfare, legality, population prevalence, or overall policy value.
