## Alignment decision

The planned article fits the call if and only if it reports the conducted formal counterfactual simulation and turns the formal invariant into practical organizational diagnostics. A formal model plus proposed research design would not satisfy the call's request for substantially developed work. The Robodebt application supplies a concrete institutional process while the paper's emerging-technology relevance rests on the transferable uncertainty-to-permission invariant and process-red-team method, not on claiming that Robodebt was a contemporary generative-AI system.
