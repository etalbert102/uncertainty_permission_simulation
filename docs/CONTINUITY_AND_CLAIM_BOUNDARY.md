## Standalone Contract

The 10,000-word article must define its hypothesis, formal constructs, representation-preservation fixture, Robodebt rationale, simulation, and limitations without requiring the completed 1,500-word essay. Intellectual continuity is preserved through claims and test logic, not sequel language.

Do not use “the precursor,” `P0-Precursor`, “as argued previously,” or defense-specific shorthand in reader-facing analytical prose. The article may cite the short essay if useful, but the argument cannot depend on it.

## Claim Types

Every material statement belongs to one type:

| Claim type | Required support | Prohibited inference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Formal | Definition, assumptions, proof sketch, finite check, or counterexample | General empirical prevalence |
| Historical process | Citation-level authoritative source anchor | Numeric parameter not in the source |
| Historical legal | Express attribution to a competent authority | Simulator-generated legality |
| Simulated structural | Frozen code/config/trace and validation receipt | Historical frequency or causal effect |
| Simulated numerical | Frozen run artifact, estimator, uncertainty, and provenance | Legal validity |
| Governance implication | Formal result plus source/simulation evidence and named tradeoff | Universal policy superiority |
| Transfer | Shared invariant and explicitly supported institutional similarity | Field equivalence or current-system prevalence |
| Attackability | Separate evidence of inducibility, observability, repeatability, and benefit | Inferring strategic exploitation from susceptibility |

## Four Attackability Fields

| Field | Question | Robodebt status in this study |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inducibility | Can an actor intentionally create or tune the uncertainty condition? | not established |
| Observability | Can the actor observe the workflow response well enough to learn the rule? | not established |
| Repeatability | Can the actor reproduce the condition with adequate reliability? | not established |
| Benefit | Does the resulting permission change materially benefit the actor? | not established |

The base vulnerability may be present without any of these fields. A red-team mutation demonstrates structural susceptibility in a declared model, not historical inducement or exploitation.

## Standalone Safeguards

The manuscript passes the standalone gate only if:

- the abstract and introduction state the full bounded hypothesis;
- the representation-preservation fixture is explained without internal IDs;
- formal authority, functional permission, and operational action are defined before use;
- Robodebt is introduced as a rule-based administrative workflow, not as modern AI;
- every acronym and policy ID is defined before reader-facing use;
- the results distinguish formal, simulated, and historical evidence;
- the conclusion states the attackability non-claim; and
- deleting every reference to the short essay would not break the article's logic.
